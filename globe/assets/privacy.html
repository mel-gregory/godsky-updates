Privacy Policy

Agent01 — God’s Eye View · provided by Hands with Purpose
Effective October 8, 2026 · Questions: call 1-855-AGENT01

This policy is written in plain language to describe how the page actually works. It is not legal advice.

What this service is

Agent01 — God’s Eye View is a free web page showing a live globe with public information about events such as outbreaks, storms, fires, earthquakes, and floods. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and nothing for sale. Voluntary tips are optional.

What we receive when you use the page

Chat messages. When you type a question in the chat, your message is sent to our backend and passed to our AI provider so it can generate a reply. Please do not put private personal details in chat messages.

Voice playback. If you tap to hear text read aloud, the text you chose to hear is sent to our text-to-speech provider to create the audio. Nothing is sent for voice unless you tap to listen.

Location. Location is used only when you tap “near me.” Your browser will ask your permission first. Your location is used in your browser to show nearby information and is not stored on our servers.

Tips

Tips are optional. If you choose to support the service with Bitcoin (BTC), the transaction itself is recorded on the public blockchain. We do not receive your name, address, or payment card information from a BTC tip, and we do not collect anything beyond the blockchain transaction details.

If you tip by card, payment is processed by Stripe. We never receive or store your card number. We keep basic records of tips only as long as needed for accounting and legal purposes. We will not sell tip records.

What stays on your device

Watchplaces and alerts. Places you save and alerts you set are stored in your own browser’s storage, on your device — not on our servers. You can remove them at any time by clearing your browser storage.

API keys you enter. If you enter an API key (for example, Esri or Mapillary), it stays in your tab’s memory only. It is never sent to us, and it is cleared when you close the tab.

Third-party data and tiles

Map tiles and live data on the globe come from third parties, such as NASA and Esri. When your browser loads tiles or data from those providers, those providers may receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) under their own privacy policies. We do not control how those providers handle that information.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or collect sign-up information.
  • We do not charge for the service or collect payment card information. Voluntary tips are optional and do not unlock any feature.
  • We do not receive your saved watchplaces, your alerts, or API keys you enter.

Changes to this policy

If how the page works changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this policy and post the new version on the page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: call Hands with Purpose at 1-855-AGENT01.
