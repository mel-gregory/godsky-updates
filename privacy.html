Privacy Policy

God Sky Network · Effective October 9, 2026 · US Only

What this service is

God Sky Network is a free US-focused service providing live public information about events within the United States such as outbreaks, storms, fires, earthquakes, floods, volcanoes, air quality, rainfall, and cloud cover. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and nothing for sale.

What we collect

We do not require accounts or collect sign-up information. The website and social media posts are publicly accessible without providing personal information.

Social media

When you interact with our posts on Facebook, Bluesky, Telegram, or other platforms (liking, commenting, sharing, following), those platforms handle your data under their own privacy policies. We do not receive personal information about you from those interactions beyond what the platforms make visible.

Website analytics

Our update pages (worldhealth.godsky.net, weather.godsky.net) do not use tracking cookies or analytics. Server logs may record standard technical information (such as IP addresses) for operational purposes.

Third-party data sources

Event data comes from US public sources including NASA, the US Geological Survey (USGS), the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), and the National Weather Service. Satellite imagery comes from NASA. When your browser loads content from those providers, they may receive standard technical information under their own privacy policies.

Voluntary tips

God Sky Network is free. You may optionally send a voluntary tip to support the service. Tips are processed by our payment provider (Stripe) — we do not see, collect, or store your payment details. Tips are not tax-deductible donations, and they do not buy anything or unlock any features or content.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use tracking cookies or advertising.
  • We do not collect payment information (the service is free).

Changes to this policy

If how the service works changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this policy and post the new version here.
